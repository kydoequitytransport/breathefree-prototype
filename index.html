When a craving hits

  1. Hold it between your lips.
  2. 6 slow breaths in through the resistance.
  3. 6 slow breaths out.
  4. Notice: the urge is already softer.

Why it works

Resistance breathing activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the same calm your brain was chasing with nicotine. Only now you're getting it from your own breath.
